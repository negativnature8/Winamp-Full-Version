## ⚡ Key Features
<a id="key-features"></a>

### Core Functionality:
- **Versatile Audio Format Support** — Compatible with formats like MP3, MP4, FLAC, AAC, WMA, and many more.
- **Customizable Interface** — Over 20 themes (skins) to personalize your player.
- **Playlist Management** — Easy drag-and-drop functionality from Windows Explorer.
- **Video Playback** — Supports various video formats including AVI, WMV, and more.
- **CD Ripping** — Rip audio CDs to AAC, WMA, WAV, or FLAC formats.

### Advanced Features:
- **Tag Editing** — Easily modify music tags for better organization.
- **Album Art Download** — Automatically fetch album covers for your music.
- **Built-in Equalizer** — Enhance sound quality with multiple presets.
- **Remote Playback** — Stream music from other computers.
- **Online Radio Access** — Tune into a wide variety of Internet radio stations.
